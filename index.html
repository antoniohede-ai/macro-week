Times are in your device's time zone. high impact · medium · lower impact (GDP, retail sales, confidence, permits, Treasury auctions) · bank holiday. Curve tab: a model output from public data, not investment advice. The trade, z-scores and half-lives are recomputed at each refresh from the last year of Treasury par yields; the headline is the most Treasury-specific CNBC story of the last 3 days at refresh time. Actuals (act ▲ above / ▼ below / = in line with forecast, ⚡ big surprise) are US releases only, from FRED once it has the new print. Auctions: Treasury coupon auctions from TreasuryDirect; cover, indirect and dealer shares (of competitive accepted) vs the last 6 auctions of the same term; strong/weak from cover ±0.10 and dealer take ∓2pp vs average; the tail vs when-issued needs paid data and isn't shown. FOMC: the latest statement from federalreserve.gov, redlined against the previous one. Charts show the last 3 years: long enough to see the post-2022 tightening and easing cycle, short enough to read on a phone. Tap or drag a chart to read any point. Sources: FRED (US BLS, BEA, Census, Fed), Statistics Canada, ONS and the Bank of England. The page refreshes daily at 07:00 from the Forex Factory weekly feed. Yields are official Treasury par yields at each day's close; changes are in basis points. Rate decisions come from the Fed, Bank of Canada and Bank of England calendars.
